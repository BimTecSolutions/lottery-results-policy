2. How We Store Your Data

Your name and device information are stored in Google Firebase Cloud Firestore, a secure cloud database provided by Google. Analytics and crash data are processed by Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics.

3. Advertising

The App displays ads served through a third-party advertising network (e.g. Google AdMob). These networks may collect your device's advertising identifier and other technical data to show relevant ads and measure ad performance. You can reset or limit ad tracking in your device's system settings (Settings → Google → Ads, on most Android devices).

4. Third-Party Services

These providers may process data according to their own privacy policies, linked above.

Your data is retained for as long as you use the App. You may request deletion of your stored data (name, device record, and associated history) at any time by contacting us — see the Contact section below. 6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can remove it.
