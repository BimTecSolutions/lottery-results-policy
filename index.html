📱 About the App

Lottery Results is a free Android app that displays official Sri Lankan lottery draw results from both the National Lotteries Board (NLB) and the Development Lotteries Board (DLB), all in one place.

The app shows the latest draw for every lottery, lets you browse full result history by date, and is designed to be fast, simple, and easy to read.

  • Latest results for all NLB and DLB lottery draws
  • Full historical result search by date
  • Clean, modern interface with dedicated NLB and DLB sections
  • Works offline for previously loaded results; live updates when connected

Disclaimer: This app is an independent results-viewing service. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by the National Lotteries Board or the Development Lotteries Board of Sri Lanka. Always verify results through official NLB/DLB channels before making any decisions based on a draw outcome.
